COMMON QUESTIONS

Credit clarity,
without confusion.

Can an incorrect entry be corrected?

If an entry is genuinely inaccurate, you can raise a dispute with the credit bureau and/or the reporting lender with supporting documents. The outcome depends on their verification.

Can anyone guarantee a CIBIL score increase?

No legitimate consultant can guarantee a particular score increase or timeline. Scores depend on the credit information reported and the bureau's calculation.

Will a low score always mean loan rejection?

Not necessarily. Each lender has its own policy and considers multiple factors. Approval, rate and amount are solely at the lender's discretion.
